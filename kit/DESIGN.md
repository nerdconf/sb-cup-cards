# Stranger Builds: design guide for your posts

Give this file and the kit folder to your AI agent (or open the images in Canva).
Ask it: "Make a post for our members about the Stranger Builds Community Cup. Follow DESIGN.md."

## The message (use these facts, nothing else)

- Stranger Builds is a Halloween hackathon by NERDCONF. Site: strangerbuilds.com
- The Community Cup ranks communities by submitted projects.
- Two steps for each member:
  1. Pick your community at app.strangerbuilds.com/profile, "Your community".
  2. Press Submit in the app before Sunday 8 November, 23:59 Argentina time (Monday 9 November, 02:59 UTC).
- No community picked = the project counts for nobody.
- One project counts once, for the community of the person who presses Submit.
- Full rules: strangerbuilds.com/rules
- Your community page: app.strangerbuilds.com/community/<your-community>

Dark background first. One highlight color per post.

## Fonts

- Headlines: Anybody Black, often Extra Expanded, uppercase, italic is fine. (Free on Google Fonts.)
- Small labels and links: Roboto Mono Medium.
- The font files are in fonts/.

## Look

- Rough, printed, comic-book Halloween. Film grain, paper texture, thick black outlines.
- Big short headline (max 6 words). Then the two steps. Then the date.
- The Duck is our mascot: a small, slightly evil duck. Use the PNGs in ducks/ as stickers, corners or peeking from an edge.
- Logo: logos/stranger-builds-logo-for-dark-bg.png on dark, logos/stranger-builds-logo-for-light-bg.png on light.

## Tone

Short, warm, a bit spooky, a bit pushy. Builders, not "participants".
Good: "Pick us. Press Submit. Sunday 23:59." / "Zero users is a bug."
Bad: long paragraphs, corporate words, "submit your application".

## Do

- Say the deadline with its time zone.
- Say both steps: pick the community AND press Submit.
- Link the community page or strangerbuilds.com.

## Don't

- Don't change, stretch or recolor the Stranger Builds logo (except the given dark/light files).
- Don't state prize amounts or numbers we did not give you; link the rules instead.
- Don't link app.strangerbuilds.com/join.
- Don't share your private organizer page link: it is only for your team.

## Sizes

- Square post: 1080 x 1080
- Story: 1080 x 1920
- Banner (X, LinkedIn): 1500 x 500

Backgrounds in these sizes are in backgrounds/.
